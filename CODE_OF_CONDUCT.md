# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BeatFlow a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy
toward other contributors. Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
