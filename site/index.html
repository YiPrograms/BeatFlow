How it works

Play normally

BeatNext resolves the current track and prepares recommendations in the background.

Browse Up Next

A large list and details panel appears to the right of Pause or successful Results screens.

Download and open

BeatNext validates the map, refreshes SongCore, and opens Solo with the requested map selected.

Developer preview

BeatNext targets Beat Saber 1.40.8_7379 with Scotland2. Quest 3 is the primary target. Quest 2, Quest 3S, and Quest Pro are compatible by design but remain unverified.
