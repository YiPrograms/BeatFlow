Meta Quest · Beat Saber 1.40.8

Play the next song you were already looking for.

BeatFlow follows the music around the song you just played, finds a confident BeatSaver map, and brings it back to Beat Saber. Anonymous Up Next works without an account; an optional Google connection adds personalized For You recommendations.

Get BeatFlow Read the open-source project

How it feels

Play normally

BeatFlow resolves the current song in the background and keeps the game’s normal controls responsive.

See what is next

Check the pause panel or the results shelf for up to three song-based map recommendations.

Choose and play

Download a map when needed, then use Beat Saber’s normal song details screen to choose a difficulty.

Preview status

BeatFlow 0.1 targets Beat Saber 1.40.8_7379 with Scotland2. Quest 3 is the primary validation target; Quest 2, Quest 3S, and Quest Pro are designed for compatibility but remain unverified until tested. See the repository README for installation, connection, troubleshooting, and development details.
